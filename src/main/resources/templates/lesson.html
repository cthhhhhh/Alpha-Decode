SLOP

AI-generated filler content often found on social media feeds. Often associated with 'brainrot' or low-effort digital spam.

Origin & Usage

The term emerged in the early 2020s to describe the tidal wave of uncurated, machine-generated imagery and text flooding digital ecosystems. It represents a shift from "human-made" bad content to "machine-automated" mediocrity designed to farm engagement.

Current Era SLOP
Past Eras Shovelware, Garbage, Junk-Mail

"Modern 'Slop' is comparable to older terms like 'Shovelware' from the gaming industry or 'Garbage' content from the early SEO-spam era of the web."
